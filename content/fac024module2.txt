%$What is AbilityOne?$%,%$A program that provides jobs for individuals who are blind or who have other disabilities by ensuring federal agencies purchase products made by them.$%
%$Who are GSA's AbilityOne partners?$%,%$The National Industries for the Blind and SourceAmerica$%
%$How many AbilityOne items are available from GSA Global Supply?$%,%$More than 3,000 AbilityOne items$%
%$When must federal agencies buy AbilityOne products?$%,%$By law, federal agencies are required to buy products made by people who are blind or have significant disabilities.$%
%$When does the AbilityOne requirement date back to?$%,%$The late 1930s$%

%$What is FSSI?$%,%$Federal Strategic Sourcing Initiative - an initiative where GSA Global Supply expands the scope of its contracts and leverages strategic sourcing vehicles to drive down pricing and delivery time.$%
%$What categories are covered under GSA's FSSI contracts?$%,%$Office supplies, Janitorial and sanitation items (JanSan), and Maintenance, repair, and operating (MRO) items$%
%$What is the intent of FSSI?$%,%$To leverage the buying power of the federal government to secure better pricing, help track spending data, and satisfy socioeconomic purchasing goals.$%
%$What is the Purchasing Channel?$%,%$FSSI contracts for office supplies, Janitorial and sanitation items, and MRO items that have been operating for several years.$%
%$What is the Requisition Channel?$%,%$Newer FSSI contract vehicles used for Global Supply requisitions.$%
%$What is the Special Order Program (SOP)?$%,%$A program that allows customers to requisition products not already under contract in the GSA Global Supply offering (both existing Part Numbers and National Stock Numbers).$%
%$What products can GSA purchase through SOP?$%,%$Only products and ancillary services within the Federal Supply Classes that GSA Global Supply manages.$%
%$What ordering methods can be used for SOP?$%,%$MILSTRIP or Military Interdepartmental Purchase Request (MIPR)$%
%$What does MILSTRIP stand for?$%,%$Military Standard Requisitioning and Issue Procedures$%
%$What does MIPR stand for?$%,%$Military Interdepartmental Purchase Request$%
%$What does GSA Global Supply handle under SOP?$%,%$End-to-end support from initial acquisition of the item to shipment at customer location and payment to the vendor.$%
%$Can SOP support be provided worldwide?$%,%$Yes, GSA can provide SOP support worldwide.$%
%$What is an example of SOP support?$%,%$A mobile incinerator (for trash) that was delivered to central Afghanistan.$%
%$What approach does GSA take with SOP requests?$%,%$GSA seeks the simplest solution - if a customer only needs items through MAS, GSA can do that, but can also manage an entire procurement if necessary.$%
%$Who can use SOP support?$%,%$Both military and civilian customers$%

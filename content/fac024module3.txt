%$What is Supply Transformation?$%,%$GSA is transforming its wholesale and retail supply program to offer customers lower costs and faster delivery worldwide. This modernization process includes acquisition, distribution, information technology, and customer service functions.$%
%$What modernization processes are included in Supply Transformation?$%,%$Acquisition, distribution, information technology, and customer service functions$%
%$What is the Transparency benefit of Supply Transformation?$%,%$IT enhancements increased the level of transparency to better see order status and tracking information on individual shipments, and comply with DLMS standards.$%
%$What does DLMS stand for?$%,%$Defense Logistics Management Systems$%
%$What is the Better Service benefit of Supply Transformation?$%,%$Achieving faster delivery for wholesale customers and more attractive retail settings worldwide by streamlining vendor partners and standardizing delivery time and packaging standards.$%
%$What is the Savings benefit of Supply Transformation?$%,%$Incorporation of FSSI Acquisition channels to lower overall product cost by leveraging the buying power of the government to negotiate better prices and save taxpayer money.$%
%$What is the DoD Compliance benefit of Supply Transformation?$%,%$Ensure GSA's timely integration with Defense Logistics Management Systems (DLMS) requirements.$%
%$What is Enhanced Efficiency in Supply Transformation?$%,%$Supply Transformation initiatives streamline processes, making it easier and faster for government agencies to procure goods and services they need.$%
%$What is the Sustainability benefit of Supply Transformation?$%,%$GSA focuses on environmentally sustainable solutions.$%
%$What is the Innovation benefit of Supply Transformation?$%,%$GSA encourages the adoption of emerging technologies and innovative practices within federal procurement.$%
%$What is the Economic Development benefit of Supply Transformation?$%,%$GSA Global Supply partners with hundreds of vendor partners, most of them small businesses, to provide a wide array of products and foster economic development while building an effective supply chain.$%
%$How many vendor partners does GSA Global Supply work with?$%,%$Hundreds of vendor partners, most of them small businesses$%
%$Does Supply Transformation change the basic ordering process?$%,%$No, GSA continues to offer simple, requisition-based ordering and whatever method customers like to place orders will still be available.$%
